I asked AI to build a working forecasting project, then challenged whether it truly respected information availability. The first prototype filtered by alert start dates but could not prove when historical records were available. We replaced it with versioned snapshots and conservative GitHub-run availability evidence. We also separated model selection, interval calibration, and final scoring, and made unknown source coverage explicit. Tests showed that changing later scoring outcomes cannot change earlier predictions or model selection. The final version is more reproducible and honest: it reports a simple baseline winning, wide uncertainty intervals, and limitations instead of claiming unsupported accuracy or completeness.
